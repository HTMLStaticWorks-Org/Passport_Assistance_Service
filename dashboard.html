Standard Passport Dossier Requirements
Identity Proof

Aadhaar card with full 12 digits visible and validated digital signature or Voter ID.

Address Proof

Electricity bill or running bank passbook statement with entries in the last 3 months.

Date of Birth Proof

Certified municipal birth certificate or 10th CBSE/ICSE board marksheet.

Annexure E Affidavit

Self-declaration on plain paper confirming citizenship and no pending legal warrants.
