Normal passport processing typically takes 15 to 20 working days with police verification conducted prior to passport dispatch. Tatkal is an expedited emergency scheme where passports are printed and dispatched within 3 to 5 working days, often with post-police verification. Tatkal requires additional documentary proof (e.g. Annexure E and two verified identity proofs) and carries a higher government fee.

Yes. Government biometric regulations mandate that every applicant (including minors) must appear in person at the Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Post Office PSK (POPSK) for live biometric capture (fingerprints and live digital photograph). PassportEase prepares your entire dossier, books your slot, and provides a comprehensive appointment slip so your visit is swift, organized, and without documentary rejection.

For standard passport renewal, you must present:
  • Your original old passport booklet (for cancellation and return).
  • Self-attested photocopies of the first two and last two pages of your old passport.
  • Current proof of residence (e.g. Aadhaar card, utility bill, or bank passbook) if your residential address has changed.
