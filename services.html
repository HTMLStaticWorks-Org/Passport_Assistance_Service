Service 02 Validity Re-issue

Passport Renewal & Re-issue Assistance

Ideal for holders of passports that have expired or are due to expire within 12 months, or applicants whose 36/60-page visa booklets are exhausted. We ensure seamless booklet replacement while ensuring all existing valid visas remain active and undamaged.

Eligibility Criteria
  • Existing passport valid for less than 1 year or expired.
  • Booklet pages exhausted for frequent flyers.
  • Change in appearance, signature, or address.
Estimated Timeline
  • Normal Track: 10 - 15 Business Days
  • Tatkal Re-issue: 3 - 5 Business Days
MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
Original Old Passport Booklet Self-Attested Copies of 1st & Last 2 Pages Current Address Proof (if updated) Validity Expiry Explanation (if expired > 3 yrs)

Service 04 Replacement & Rectification

Lost or Damaged Passport Guidance

Navigating a lost, misplaced, water-damaged, or torn passport can be legally delicate. We assist you with registering a police Missing Article / Non-Cognizable Report (NCR), preparing Annexure F declarations, and requesting duplicate booklet issuance.

Conditions Covered
  • Passport completely lost, stolen, or misplaced.
  • Passport mutilated beyond readability.
  • Partial damage (e.g., covers detached, water soaked).
Estimated Timeline
  • Estimated: 20 - 30 Business Days
  • Police Verification: Mandatory prior clearance
MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
Police Lost Certificate / Online NCR Annexure F Affidavit regarding circumstances of loss Damaged Booklet (if damaged rather than lost) Copy of previous passport (if available)
Lost Passport Facilitation Package ₹4,499 (Includes ₹3,000 Govt Penalty Fee + ₹1,499 Assistance)

Service 06 Ages 0 to 18

Minor Passport Assistance (Under 18 Years)

Applying for infants and children under 18 involves unique parental consent protocols. We handle Annexure D declarations, single-parent consent affidavits (Annexure C), child birth certification, and school bonafide records.

Validity Rules
  • Children aged 0 - 15: Issued 5-year validity.
  • Teens aged 15 - 18: Can opt for 10-year adult validity.
  • Both parents' consent or legal custody order required.
Estimated Timeline
  • Estimated: 12 - 18 Business Days
  • Verification: Often exempt from physical police verification
MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
Certified Municipal Birth Certificate Passports of Both Parents (Self-attested copies) Parental Consent Affidavit (Annexure D) Parent Present Address Proof
