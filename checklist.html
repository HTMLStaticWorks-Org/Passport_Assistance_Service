Expert Submission Guidelines & Pitfalls to Avoid
1. Exact Name Matching: Ensure the applicant's name matches character-by-character across your Aadhaar, 10th marksheet, and birth certificate. Even an expanded initials discrepancy can trigger an Annexure E requirement.
2. Clear Scan Legibility: Scans must be 300 DPI color copies with all 4 corners visible. Black-and-white photocopies with cut edges or blurred municipal seals will be returned by the verification officer.
3. Proof of Address Recency: Utility bills (Electricity, Piped Gas, Water) and bank passbook statements must have entries within the preceding 90 days. Older bills are not admissible.
4. Minor Parental Passports: If either parent holds an existing passport, their spouse name must be formally endorsed in the other spouse’s booklet before applying for a child's passport.
